non-commercial permission. Production xG/xT work uses StatsBomb open data and credits StatsBomb. These match

Production motion assets are derived from Mixamo under its applicable terms; no Mixamo source animations or
derived motion data are distributed here. The public synthetic preview uses hand-authored poses.

The README images are screenshots of the demonstration. Logos identify their respective owners and acknowledge
the components used; they do not indicate endorsement. Permission and attribution for the final submission

the trained expected-threat model.
