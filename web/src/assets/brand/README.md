# YIZHAN brand marks

Master artwork copied unchanged from `yizhan-website/brand/` (YIZHAN-06, 16 September 2026).
Rules from the identity guidelines v2.0, which the stadium sign follows:

- Two formats only: the symbol, or the horizontal signature. Never separate or rearrange them.
- Black (#000000) or white (#FFFFFF) only, at full opacity. No other colour, tint or gradient.
- On a surface you do not control, set the mark on a solid black or white panel.
- Clear space of at least 0.25 H on every side, where H is the visible symbol height.
- Scale the master artwork; never redraw it.

The slogan, "Intelligence takes flight.", is set as on yizhan.dev, in Jost (SIL Open Font
License, `fonts/OFL-Jost.txt`), white on black on the goal-end LED boards.

`kick-mark-white.png` is the Project KICK monogram, the pure icon without the "PROJ. KICK" lettering: white on
transparent, trimmed from `<private-workspace>` (itself cropped from
`kick_site/assets/logo_wordmark.png` at the mark's alpha). On the LED boards it stands beside "PROJECT KICK" set in Jost
(Yi, 2026-10-23), white on black.
